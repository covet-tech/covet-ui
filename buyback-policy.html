The fine print

Our instant buyback,
in plain words.

Every reveal comes with an offer to sell the piece straight back to us. Here is exactly how that offer works.

Effective August 2026 · Questions? The live support button in the footer reaches a real human.

What the buyback is

When a box reveals your piece, we show an instant repurchase offer next to it. Accept it and the amount lands in your Covet balance immediately as credit. Decline it and the piece is yours: ship it free or let it wait on your shelf, where a current offer stays available.

How offers are set

Offers are set per item, in whole dollars, and shown before you decide anything. Offers are always below the listed market value, and we set them at our discretion. Offers can change at any time as market conditions change. Accepting an offer at the reveal locks it in; an item waiting on your shelf carries whatever the current offer is when you sell it, which may be higher or lower than the offer at reveal.

Free and promotional boxes

Buyback offers on free and promotional pulls are reduced and capped. Everyday free pulls carry small fixed offers, usually between $0.50 and $2. Rare free pulls carry larger offers, set per item. The offer is always shown on the reveal before you choose.

What buyback credit is

Buyback credit is Covet balance. It spends on any box. Credit from free and promotional pulls spends on boxes only until it has been used in a purchase; after that it counts toward bank transfers like any other balance. Bank transfers start at $20, unlock after your first purchase, require one identity verification, and take 3 to 5 business days. Transfer fees may apply.

What the buyback is not

It is never an obligation. You can always keep your piece. It is not a price guarantee on market value, and it is not cash at the moment of sale; it is store credit with a path to your bank under the conditions above.
