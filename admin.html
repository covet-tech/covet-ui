Maintenance mode

Pauses the whole storefront behind a "be right back" page while you fix or restock. Balances, shelves, and streaks are untouched; the admin panel stays reachable. In production this is a server flag, so reveals already in motion finish before the curtain drops.

"Fulfill via API" places the supplier order and attaches tracking automatically. "Manual" records your source and tracking number. "Credit" credits her balance instead of shipping.

Every accepted buyback is an order you never had to fulfill. Watch for heavy cyclers: many buybacks plus a withdrawal request is the pattern worth a second look.

You never approve routine payments. Deposits and purchases are fully automatic, and verified users auto-pay transfers under $500 with no review. Only three things wait here for your click: an account's first-ever withdrawal, flagged accounts, and transfers of $500 or more. Creator earnings are the exception to the exception: once a creator is verified, their earnings payouts auto-pay at any amount, since commission money was already vetted when it accrued and carries no chargeback risk. Their first payout still gets your eyes once.

Instant buyback dial

What we offer on paid-box pulls, as a % of what WE source the item for (supplier cost in production, sourcing-class estimate in the demo). At 100% a sell-back costs you exactly a restock. Start lean while cash is tight, then turn it up: richer offers drive sell-backs and the credit re-spends at your edge. The free-rares dial covers rare pulls from free boxes, offered near our fulfillment cost since shipping would cost us 100% anyway. Neither dial touches free-box penny commons or items with an explicit buyback price.

Preset boxes

Every box on the site, editable. The starter boxes are placeholders; edit any of them (or author new ones) and your version replaces it. Odds must total 100 and the EV meter must sit in the green band before Save unlocks.

Daily free boxes

The boxes in the daily free rotation. Edit items, odds, and buyback offers, turn any off, or add a fresh one to swap the rotation; the home page shows whatever is on. Free boxes have no price, so Save only needs odds totaling 100.

The Shop

Buy-now storefront, linked from the footer. Add any catalog or custom item and set its shop price. Anchor rule: shop prices sit AT OR ABOVE the item's shown market value, or the box value story collapses. All sales final; damaged or wrong arrivals always made right.

Homepage hero cards

The three product cards floating out of the hero gift box, and the second set they rotate to. Pick the photo and edit the name and the little tag bubble under it; "hot" makes the bubble pink.

Item catalog

Find any item across every box. Edit its shown value when the market moves, pause it while restocking (it can't be pulled, so nobody needs crediting), or remove it for good (the box's remaining odds re-balance to 100).
