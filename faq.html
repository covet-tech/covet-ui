Help · quick answers

Asked and answered.

The short version of everything. Anything else, the live support button in the footer reaches a real human.

Is everything actually real?

Yes, always. Every piece is verified authentic before it's ever listed. A dupe has never touched a Covet box and never will. That promise is the whole brand, and your pull arrives sealed with its paperwork where applicable.

How do the odds work, and can I check them?

Every box prints its full odds table before you spend a cent: every possible piece, its market value, and its exact chance, always totaling 100%. Every reveal is verifiable pull by pull, and the full methodology lives in the odds disclosure. What you see is what runs.

What's the instant buyback?

Not your vibe? On every reveal we offer to repurchase your pull on the spot for the amount shown. Accept and the credit lands in your balance instantly, ready for your next box. Free-box pulls carry smaller offers, because those are made for keeping.

How does shipping work?

Free, every order, no minimum. Your pull ships with tracking within 5 business days, and pieces over $500 ship insured with signature on delivery. Pulls wait on your shelf as long as you like. We ship to all US addresses, Puerto Rico included. One carrier quirk: perfume travels by ground under federal safety rules, so fragrance to the mainland arrives in the normal window, and fragrance to Puerto Rico can take longer. Everything else is unaffected.

What's the free daily box?

One free box every single day. Your first 3 are on us before you ever buy anything; open any box, even the $10 one, and the daily free box unlocks forever. Pick one of the day's options, open it, and a real piece lands on your shelf. Keep it and your first one ships free (after that, free-pull shipping unlocks with your first purchase), or take a small credit toward your next box. Opening daily builds your streak, and every 7th day banks bonus giveaway entries ♥

How do I enter the weekly giveaway?

Every $1 you open earns 1 entry, 5 free entries wait for you daily, following @covet.gg adds 3 per platform, and posting the giveaway adds 5 more. No purchase is ever necessary; there's a free entry route in the rules. One winner is drawn every Sunday at midnight ET by random.org, with the signed draw linked from the announcement.

What's a wishlist box?

Your own box, built by you. Heart 2 to 8 pieces you're eyeing, slide each one's chance (they always total 100%), and unbox it. One of your picks ships to you. Name it, save it, and keep as many custom boxes as you like.

How does my balance work?

Your balance holds deposits and buyback credit, and spends on any box. Credit from free boxes spends on boxes first; once you've used it in an open, it counts toward transfers. Transfers back to your bank start at $20, unlock after your first purchase, and take a one-time ID check, then 3 to 5 business days to land. Transfer fees may apply.

Can I get a refund?

Box opens are final: you received a real piece and a buyback offer the moment it revealed. Money you've deposited but not spent is always refundable to your original payment method; just message live support. Buyback credit isn't refunded; it transfers to your bank through the normal way.

Why do I have to verify my ID?

Once, quickly, with our verification partner, before your first free box or bank transfer. It keeps the free boxes and the giveaway fair for everyone. Your ID is never stored on Covet, and we won't ask again.

Who can use Covet?

You must be 18 or older (19+ in AL and NE) and located in a US state where randomized retail products are permitted. A few states aren't available yet; the gate on your first visit checks eligibility.
